How Pennsylvania Taxes 1099 Freelancers & S-Corps in 2026

Frequently Asked Questions: Pennsylvania 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

Do Pennsylvania S-Corp K-1 distributions avoid local municipal Earned Income Tax (EIT)?

Yes! Under Pennsylvania Act 32, local municipal EIT (1%–3% outside Philadelphia) taxes W-2 wages and Schedule C net profit, NOT S-Corp K-1 distributions—an extra bonus for PA S-Corp owners.

How much is Pennsylvania's annual LLC or S-Corp report fee?

Just $7/year with the Pennsylvania Department of State, with $0 state minimum franchise tax.

Does Pennsylvania recognize federal IRS Form 2553 automatically?

Yes. Federal S-Corporations are automatically recognized as PA S-Corporations unless you formally opt out.
