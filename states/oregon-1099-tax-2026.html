Frequently Asked Questions: Oregon 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

How much is the Oregon S-Corp minimum excise tax in 2026?

Oregon charges a flat $150 minimum annual excise tax on S-Corporations (Form OR-20-S) plus $100/year for the Secretary of State renewal.

Does Oregon have a Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) SALT cap workaround?

Yes! Oregon's PTE-E election lets S-Corps pay Oregon's 9%–9.9% tax at the business level and deduct it on federal Form 1120-S.

Do Oregon freelancers pay the Corporate Activity Tax (CAT)?

Only if annual Oregon commercial activity (gross receipts) exceeds $1,000,000.
