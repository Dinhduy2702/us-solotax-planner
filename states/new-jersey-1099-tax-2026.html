Frequently Asked Questions: New Jersey 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

Do I still need to file Form CBT-2553 for a New Jersey S-Corp in 2026?

No. New Jersey repealed the separate CBT-2553 requirement—businesses with a valid federal IRS Form 2553 election are automatically treated as NJ S-Corps.

What is the minimum annual tax for a New Jersey S-Corp?

New Jersey charges a graduated CBT minimum tax starting at $375–$500 for small S-Corps with gross receipts under $250,000, plus a $75 annual report.

What is New Jersey's BAIT election for S-Corps?

BAIT (Business Alternative Income Tax) lets NJ S-Corps and partnerships pay NJ income tax at the business level, turning it into a federal business deduction that bypasses the $10,000 SALT cap.
