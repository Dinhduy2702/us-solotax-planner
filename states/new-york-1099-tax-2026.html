How New York Taxes 1099 Freelancers & S-Corps in 2026

New York freelancers face a tale of two completely different tax regimes in 2026 depending on whether you live outside or inside New York City. New York State recognizes federal S-Corp status (after filing separate NY Form CT-6) and charges a modest Fixed Dollar Minimum fee ($25 to $4,500 based on NY receipts—typically $25–$300 for solo consultants), plus 4%–10.9% personal income tax. However, New York City does NOT recognize S-Corporation status at all!

Real New York LLC vs. S-Corp Break-Even Analysis

Inside the five boroughs of NYC, a Sole Proprietor pays NYC's 4% Unincorporated Business Tax (UBT, with an exemption credit below ~$100,000 net income), whereas an NYC S-Corp is hit with NYC's 8.85% General Corporation Tax (GCT) on corporate net income—which can wipe out federal S-Corp savings if your K-1 distribution is too large! Outside NYC (Long Island, Westchester, Upstate NY), a standard New York S-Corp works cleanly: on $120,000 net profit, the $300 NY fixed fee leaves strong net federal SE tax savings after payroll and CPA fees.

4 Legal Ways New York Solopreneurs Cut 1099 Taxes in 2026

  • Outside NYC: file BOTH federal IRS Form 2553 AND New York State Form CT-6 to elect NY S-Corp status—missing Form CT-6 causes NY to tax you as a C-Corp!
  • Inside NYC: consult a NYC CPA before electing S-Corp status because NYC's 8.85% corporate tax on K-1 profit can erase federal FICA savings unless officer salary and Solo 401(k) deductions are carefully calibrated.
  • Remember NYC Sole Proprietors owe 0% UBT below ~$100k net profit, and New York State S-Corps can elect NY's Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) to bypass the $10,000 federal SALT cap.
  • Max out Solo 401(k) and HSA deductions—both reduce Federal, New York State, AND NYC local personal income taxes simultaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions: New York 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

Does New York City recognize S-Corporation tax status in 2026?

No. New York City treats S-Corps like regular C-Corporations under the 8.85% General Corporation Tax (GCT), making S-Corp planning inside the five boroughs very different from the rest of New York State.

Do I need to file a separate New York form to become an S-Corp?

Yes. In addition to federal IRS Form 2553, you must file New York Form CT-6 to be recognized as a New York State S-Corporation.

What is the New York State annual fee for a solo S-Corp?

New York charges a Fixed Dollar Minimum fee based on NY gross receipts—typically $25 (under $100k receipts), $50 ($100k–$250k), or $175–$300 ($250k–$500k).
