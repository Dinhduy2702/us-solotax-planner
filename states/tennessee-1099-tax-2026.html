How Tennessee Taxes 1099 Freelancers & S-Corps in 2026

Tennessee is a famous 0% personal income tax state—but it hides a CRITICAL S-Corp and LLC tax trap that catches relocating freelancers off guard in 2026! Unlike Texas or Florida, Tennessee levies a Franchise & Excise (F&E) Tax on ALL LLCs and S-Corporations (6.5% Excise Tax on net earnings ABOVE Tennessee's $50,000 standard excise exemption, plus a 0.25% Franchise Tax / $100 min + $300 annual report)—whereas unincorporated Sole Proprietors pay $0 F&E tax!

Real Tennessee LLC vs. S-Corp Break-Even Analysis

Here is the brilliant Tennessee S-Corp math: because Tennessee grants a $50,000 standard excise deduction AND lets an S-Corp deduct your reasonable W-2 officer salary (e.g., $54,000) from corporate net earnings, a Tennessee S-Corp earning $120,000 net profit has only ~$12,000–$16,000 of taxable excise earnings left after W-2 + $50k exemption—cutting Tennessee's 6.5% Excise Tax down to ~$1,000–$1,500 (including the $300 annual report)! That means at $120,000 profit, a Tennessee S-Corp STILL beats a Sole Proprietorship by ~$4,100+/year—while paying 0% personal income tax!

4 Legal Ways Tennessee Solopreneurs Cut 1099 Taxes in 2026
