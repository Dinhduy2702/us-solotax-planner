How Ohio Taxes 1099 Freelancers & S-Corps in 2026

Any business profit above $250,000 is capped at a flat 3.0%.

Frequently Asked Questions: Ohio 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

How does Ohio's $250,000 Business Income Deduction work for 1099 freelancers?

Ohio exempts the first $250,000 of net Schedule C, LLC, or S-Corp K-1 business income ($125,000 Married Filing Separately) from Ohio state income tax—meaning 0% Ohio state tax on $120,000 of profit!

How much is the annual fee for an Ohio LLC or S-Corp?

$0! Ohio does not charge an annual report or minimum franchise tax fee for LLCs or S-Corporations.

Do Ohio S-Corp owners still pay Ohio Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)?

No—Ohio exempted businesses with under $6 million in annual Ohio gross receipts from the CAT.
