Frequently Asked Questions: Michigan 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

How much is the annual fee for a Michigan LLC or S-Corp in 2026?

Just $25/year to the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA), with $0 minimum franchise tax.

Does Michigan recognize federal IRS Form 2553 automatically?

Yes—no separate Michigan S-Corp election form is required.

What is Michigan's Flow-Through Entity (FTE) tax election?

It lets Michigan S-Corps pay Michigan's 4.25% tax at the business level, generating a full federal business deduction that bypasses the $10,000 SALT cap.
