Frequently Asked Questions: Maryland 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

How much is Maryland's annual LLC or S-Corp fee in 2026?

$300/year paid to Maryland SDAT (State Department of Assessments and Taxation) with Form 1, due April 15.

Does Maryland's PTET election cover both State and County income taxes?

Yes! Maryland's Pass-Through Entity Tax election lets S-Corps pay both the state rate and the top county rate at the entity level for a full federal deduction.

Does Maryland require a separate state S-Corp election form?

No. Maryland automatically recognizes your federal IRS Form 2553 S-Corp election.
