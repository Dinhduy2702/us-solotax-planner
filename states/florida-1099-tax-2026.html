How Florida Taxes 1099 Freelancers & S-Corps in 2026

Florida is a top-tier home for 1099 freelancers, online creators, and S-Corporation owners in 2026. With 0% personal state income tax and 0% state corporate tax on S-Corp pass-through profits, Florida solopreneurs pay only a flat ~$138.75–$150 annual Sunbiz report fee to keep their LLC or S-Corp active—no state quarterly estimated income taxes and no gross receipts tax.

Frequently Asked Questions: Florida 1099 & S-Corp Taxes

Does Florida charge corporate income tax on S-Corporations?

No. Florida S-Corporations that pass income through to shareholders owe $0 in Florida corporate or personal income tax.

What is the annual cost to maintain a Florida LLC or S-Corp?

Florida charges $138.75/yr for LLCs ($150/yr for corporations) via Sunbiz.org, due between January 1 and May 1 each year.

Do Florida 1099 freelancers need to pay state estimated taxes?

No. Florida has 0% personal income tax, so freelancers only make quarterly federal IRS Form 1040-ES payments.
